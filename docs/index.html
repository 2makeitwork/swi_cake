swi_iceberg

Compare many distributions side by side. Each object's samples are summarized around their median, scaled by MADN (the median absolute deviation times 1.4826, a robust stand-in for the standard deviation) into six bands from −3 to +3; a band's width is how many samples fall in it, its height is the value range those samples span.

Explore

  • Interactive fleet page Twelve example objects, pan and zoom, per-band tooltips, view toggles, and a "Random fleet" button that swaps in another pre-computed fleet.
  • Large fleet, ultra compact view 300 objects drawn as one-pixel-wide spines per band.
